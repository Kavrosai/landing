Agents built for you — and governed from the first invocation

If the reason your AI-agent work hasn't shipped is that nobody on your team builds agents, that part is ours to fix: we write the agent or workflow for you, and it runs on Kavros — a governance plane deployed in your AWS account, where every action is checked against a signed scope, screened for sensitive data before anything leaves, recorded in tamper-evident records your auditors can read, and stoppable mid-run without a redeploy.

Who this is for

You don't need an in-house agent team. Bring the recurring work you'd want an agent to own — intake triage, expense or invoice workflows, status reporting, anything your security review would otherwise have to bless — and we build it: the workflow, the tool scopes, the policy, the evidence path. You own the deployment; it runs inside your AWS account, not a vendor's SaaS.

If your team can build agents themselves — that's fine too, and we won't take it personally: the same boundary works for agents you write yourself. Governance is a property of the plane, not of who wrote the agent.

What Kavros claims — and what it does not

Security buyers should distrust vendors who skip this section.

Kavros claims:

  • The governance boundary described above is shipped behavior: Ed25519-signed per-agent policies evaluated at enforcement, pre-egress DLP, hash-chained allow/block records, and an out-of-process halt that revokes credentials mid-run. The same boundary the rest of this site describes.
  • Agents we build for you are built and customized by the founder personally — no sales engineer between you and the code.
  • Custom code runs inside the boundary: tool access via grants, outbound payloads through DLP, and every decision recorded — designed invariants, not optional manners.

Kavros does not claim:

  • No "platform updates can never break your custom code." The substrate-update loop in the diagram is a design with a conformance suite behind it — it ships as fact when one real agent survives one real core update in one real customer account. Ask us for the recorded run.
  • No plugin SDK, marketplace, or third-party plugin ecosystem exists yet. This offer is founder-built agents on a governed substrate, nothing more.
  • No compliance certification is claimed. The boundary produces evidence exports that map onto your SOC 2 / ISO 42001 / EU AI Act programs; it does not make you compliant.
  • Governance is a ceiling, not a guarantee — the same caveat everywhere else on this site. An agent that is authorized to touch a system can still do damage inside that authorization; the scopes and review process are yours to set.
